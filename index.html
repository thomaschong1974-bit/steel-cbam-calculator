② 七条生产路线② The Seven Production Routes

钢铁免费配额基准全欧统一，碳钢 C/D/E、合金钢(非不锈) F/G/H/J。路线不由用户随意选，而是按原产国确定：IR 2025/2620 §5.1 规定"须采用 2621 附录I 中该原产国对应的生产路线"，源表每国分表第9列即给出该国该 CN 的指定路线（如中国半成品为 (C) BF/BOF，废钢电炉为主的国家则为 (E)）。本计算器据此执行双态逻辑: 默认值路径下路线由法规锁定(不可改); 实测路径下解锁, 由使用者按本厂实际生产路线选择, 提示条会同时显示该国法规默认作对照。Free-allocation benchmarks are EU-wide: carbon C/D/E, alloy (non-stainless) F/G/H/J. The route is not a free user choice — it is fixed by country of origin: IR 2025/2620 §5.1 requires "the production route indicated in Annex I to IR 2025/2621 for the country of origin", given in column 9 of each country sheet (e.g. China semis → (C) BF/BOF; scrap-EAF economies → (E)). The calculator applies a two-state logic: on the default path the route is locked by regulation; on the actual path it unlocks for you to pick your installation's real route, with the origin's regulatory default shown alongside for reference.

关于 (1)(2)：IR 2025/2620 §5.3 明确：(1) 用于生产年份 2026-27、(2) 用于 2028-30。基准值本身按年份分两档（例如镍铁 7202 60 00 = 2.390(1) / 2.295(2)），本模型随所选年份自动切换。另有一条独立规则（§5.1）：同一 CN 若并列多个合金牌号，取"当年"最高值。On (1)/(2): IR 2025/2620 §5.3 states (1) applies to production years 2026-27 and (2) to 2028-30. Benchmarks are split into these two year bands (e.g. ferro-nickel 7202 60 00 = 2.390(1) / 2.295(2)); the model switches automatically with the selected year. A separate rule (§5.1) says that where a CN lists several alloy grades, the highest value for the relevant year is used.

基准值 BenchmarksBenchmarks

来自 IR 2025/2620, 全欧统一、按『CN 码 × 生产路线』给出(碳钢 C/D/E、合金 F/G/H/J), 代表欧盟高效产能水平, 用于计算可免费扣减的部分。三个要点与本工具一一对应: ① 路线不由用户任选, 按原产国自动取 2621 附录I 指定路线; ② 基准分 (1) 2026-27 与 (2) 2028-30 两个年份档, 随申报年份切换; ③ 分两列——A 列=仅本工序(官方规定采实测数据时按式2逐工序用), B 列=含前驱体(采默认值时用)。本工具两条路径均以 B 列近似, 严格的实测申报应按 A 列逐工序累加, 此为已披露的简化。各码 A 列官方数值已收录于⑫数据速查的审计附表。可扣减量 = 基准 × 免费配额系数 × CSCF, 系数由 2026 年 97.5% 退坡至 2034 年 0%, CSCF 未公布前取 1。From IR 2025/2620, EU-wide per CN code × production route (carbon C/D/E, alloy F/G/H/J), representing efficient EU capacity and setting the free-allocation deduction. Three points mirror this tool: ① the route is not a free choice — it is auto-set from the origin per 2621 Annex I; ② benchmarks come in two year bands, (1) 2026-27 and (2) 2028-30, switched by declaration year; ③ two columns — A = process-only (officially used process-by-process with actual data, Eq. 2), B = incl. precursors (used with defaults). This tool approximates both paths with Column B; strict actual-data filings accumulate Column A per process — a disclosed simplification. Official Column A values per code are provided in the audit appendix under the Data tables section. Deduction = benchmark × free-allocation factor × CSCF; the factor phases from 97.5% (2026) to 0% (2034); CSCF = 1 until published.

实行时间: CBAM 过渡期 2023-10 至 2025-12 (仅申报); 正式期自 2026-01 起产生财务义务; 证书自 2027-02-01 起开售 (Omnibus Reg 2025/2083 推迟); 首次申报须在 2027-09-30 前(覆盖 2026 年, 原 5-31 经 Omnibus 延后); 免费配额 2034 年归零。钢铁保障配额新规 (Reg 2026/1384) 自 2026-07 起与 CBAM 并行。Timing: transitional 2023-10 to 2025-12 (reporting only); definitive from 2026-01 (financial obligation); certificate sales open 2027-02-01 (postponed by Omnibus Reg 2025/2083); first declaration by 2027-09-30 (for 2026; moved from 31 May); free allocation reaches 0% in 2034. The new steel safeguard quotas (Reg 2026/1384) run in parallel from 2026-07.

⑤ 什么是 CBAM? (钢铁视角)⑤ What is CBAM? (steel perspective)

欧盟碳边境调节机制 (CBAM) 对进口的钢铁等高碳产品, 按其隐含碳排放征收与欧盟碳市场 (EU ETS) 挂钩的费用, 使进口钢材与欧盟本地生产承担同等碳成本, 防止『碳泄漏』。The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) charges imported carbon-intensive goods (including iron & steel) for their embedded emissions, priced against the EU ETS, so imported steel bears the same carbon cost as EU production and prevents carbon leakage.

对钢铁而言: ①范围为 CN 7201–7326 的钢铁产品(不锈钢 7218–7223 有专属基准, 本工具聚焦非不锈); ②进口人须申报每批货的隐含排放, 可用官方默认值(简便、含上浮)或经核证的实测值(通常更低); ③按路线基准×免费配额系数扣减免费配额部分; ④就应清缴部分购买 CBAM 证书, 价格挂钩 EU ETS; ⑤境外已付的碳价可抵扣。For steel: (1) scope is CN 7201–7326 (stainless 7218–7223 have their own benchmarks; this tool focuses on non-stainless); (2) importers declare embedded emissions per shipment, using official defaults (simple, with mark-up) or verified actual data (usually lower); (3) the free-allocation share (route benchmark × factor) is deducted; (4) certificates are bought for the chargeable part at the EU ETS-linked price; (5) carbon price already paid abroad is deductible.

钢铁在定义期只计『直接排放』; 间接(电力/Scope 2)仅过渡期报告, 不计入 CBAM 成本。(进口商指南 5.6.1 脚注41)For iron & steel the definitive period counts direct emissions only; indirect (electricity/Scope 2) is transitional-reporting only. (Guidance 5.6.1, footnote 41)

⑩ 计算方法论⑩ Calculation methodology

整个计算只有两条公式、五个步骤。每一步的数字都有官方出处, 与上方计算器的"自动取值面板"逐项对应。The whole calculation is two formulas in five steps. Every figure has an official source and maps one-to-one to the auto-values panel above.

Echarge = max( SEE − BM × F × CSCF , 0 )
每吨成本Cost/t = Echarge × max( Pcert − Ppaid × cov , 0 )
第1步 · 选用内含排放 SEE (tCO₂e/吨)Step 1 · Selected embedded emissions, SEE (tCO₂e/t)
二选一。默认值路径: 按『原产国 × CN 编码』查官方默认直接排放, 再乘当年上浮 (2026 +10% / 2027 +20% / 2028起 +30%); 实测路径: 用经核证的实测排放, 不加上浮。钢铁在正式期只计直接排放。出处: IR 2025/2621 · TAXUD 指南 §5.6Choose one. Default path: official default direct emissions by origin × CN, times the year's mark-up (+10% 2026 / +20% 2027 / +30% from 2028); Actual path: verified actual emissions, no mark-up. Steel counts direct emissions only in the definitive period. Source: IR 2025/2621 · TAXUD guidance §5.6
第2步 · 确定基准值 BM (tCO₂e/吨)Step 2 · Determine the benchmark, BM (tCO₂e/t)
工具自动完成三连跳: 原产国+CN → 该国法规指定的生产路线 (默认值路径锁定; 实测路径可按实际路线选择) → 该 CN 该路线的基准值 (B列, 含前驱体) → 按申报年份取 (1) 2026-27 档或 (2) 2028-30 档。不分路线的品目直接取单一基准。出处: IR 2025/2620 §5.1·§5.3 · 2621 附录IAuto-derived in three hops: origin + CN → the route the regulation fixes for that country (locked on the default path; selectable to your real route on the actual path) → that CN-route benchmark (Column B, incl. precursors) → year band (1) 2026-27 or (2) 2028-30. Route-independent codes use a single benchmark. Source: IR 2025/2620 §5.1·§5.3 · 2621 Annex I
第3步 · 免费配额扣减 (tCO₂e/吨)Step 3 · Free-allocation adjustment (tCO₂e/t)
可免费扣减 = BM × F × CSCF。F = 免费配额系数, 随 EU ETS 免费配额退坡: 2026 年 97.5% → 逐年下降 → 2034 年 0%; CSCF = 跨行业修正系数, 官方未公布前取 1。出处: IR 2025/2620 式6 · ETS指令 Art 10a(1a)Deduction = BM × F × CSCF. F = free-allocation factor, phasing down with EU ETS free allocation: 97.5% in 2026 → 0% in 2034; CSCF = cross-sectoral correction factor, 1 until published. Source: IR 2025/2620 Eq. 6 · ETS Directive Art 10a(1a)
第4步 · 应清缴排放与证书量Step 4 · Chargeable emissions & certificates
每吨应清缴 E_charge = max( SEE − 扣减 , 0 ), 不会为负; 应购证书总量 = E_charge × 数量(吨)。1 张证书 = 1 吨 CO₂e, 实际清缴按整数张——本工具显示四舍五入整张数并保留精确吨数, 尾数取整规则以 CBAM Registry 申报界面为准。出处: Reg 2023/956Chargeable per tonne E_charge = max( SEE − deduction , 0 ), floored at zero; total certificates = E_charge × quantity (t). One certificate = 1 tCO2e and surrender is in whole certificates - the tool shows the rounded whole number alongside the exact tonnage; the Registry's own rounding prevails. Source: Reg 2023/956
第5步 · 成本 (欧元)Step 5 · Cost (EUR)
每吨成本 = E_charge × max( 证书价 − 境外已付碳价 × 覆盖比例 , 0 ); 总成本 = 每吨成本 × 数量。证书价为欧委会公布的 ETS 拍卖均价 (2026 年按季度: Q1 €75.36 · Q2 €75.28), 未公布时段按最新已公布价估算并标注。出处: Reg 2023/956 Art 9·21 · EC 证书价格页Cost/t = E_charge × max( certificate price − foreign carbon price paid × coverage , 0 ); total = cost/t × quantity. The certificate price is the Commission-published ETS auction average (quarterly for 2026: Q1 €75.36 · Q2 €75.28); unpublished periods are estimated at the latest published price and labelled. Source: Reg 2023/956 Art 9·21 · EC price page
模型假设与出处 (审计口径)Model assumptions & sources (audit basis)
① 免费配额扣减完整公式为 SEFA = CBAM系数 × CSCF × BM_B (IR 2025/2620 附录式6)；其中 CSCF (跨行业修正系数) 2021-2025 官方为 100%；2026-2030 欧盟已设约 4.5 亿配额缓冲、机制设计上避免触发 CSCF（即预期维持 100%），本模型默认取 1，若欧委会另行公布可在高级参数中修改。② CBAM系数按 ETS指令 Art 10a(1a)：97.5%/95%/90%/77.5%/51.5%/…/0%。③ 证书价格 = 欧委会按 EU ETS 拍卖均价公布 (Omnibus 后 2026 年为季度均价: Q1 €75.36、Q2 €75.28 均为官方已公布值)，其余时段为估算，申报以官方公布为准。④ 钢铁定义期只计直接排放 (TAXUD 指南脚注41)。按 IR 2026/1740, 附录不再印含上浮列, 最终含上浮值由 CBAM Registry 按总排放DV 计算；全部 72/73 码间接排放均为 N/A, 总排放=直接排放, 本工具口径一致且上浮只乘一次。⑤ 境外碳价抵扣 = 已付碳价 × 覆盖比例 (高级参数可调, 默认100%)，为简化口径；其实施细则 2026-05-13 草案已结束征求意见、尚未通过, 正式折算方法以通过后的条例为准。① Full deduction formula: SEFA = CBAM-factor × CSCF × BM_B (IR 2025/2620 Annex Eq. 6); the CSCF (cross-sectoral correction factor) was 100% for 2021-2025; for 2026-2030 the EU has set a ~450 M-allowance buffer designed to avoid triggering it (i.e. expected to stay 100%) — this model defaults to 1, adjustable under Advanced parameters if the Commission publishes otherwise. ② CBAM factor per ETS Directive Art 10a(1a): 97.5%/95%/90%/77.5%/51.5%/…/0%. ③ Certificate price = Commission-published EU ETS auction average (quarterly for 2026 post-Omnibus: Q1 €75.36 and Q2 €75.28 are official published values); other periods are estimates — declarations must use official figures. ④ Steel definitive period counts direct emissions only (TAXUD guidance fn. 41). Per IR 2026/1740 the annex no longer prints marked-up columns — final marked-up values are computed in the CBAM Registry from the total-emissions DV; all 72/73 codes show indirect = N/A, so total = direct, matching this tool, with the mark-up applied exactly once. ⑤ Foreign-carbon-price offset = price paid × coverage share (adjustable, default 100%) — a simplification; the implementing act (draft of 13 May 2026, consultation closed) is not yet adopted, and the definitive method will follow the adopted regulation.

注: SEE = Specific Embedded Emissions (单位内含排放)。基准值 B 列已按前驱体累加法折算 (IR 2025/2547): 产品嵌入 = Σ(前驱体用量 × 前驱体嵌入) + 本工序直接排放, 因此默认值路径下无需自行累加前驱体。Note: SEE = Specific Embedded Emissions. Column-B benchmarks already embed precursor accumulation (IR 2025/2547): product embed = Σ(precursor use × precursor embed) + this-stage direct — so no manual precursor roll-up is needed on the default path.

⑪ 上浮与 CBAM factor · 年度表⑪ Mark-up & CBAM factor · yearly schedule

『上浮』只在使用默认值时叠加(实测值不加), 逐年提高以推动企业转向实测; 『免费配额系数(CBAM factor)』决定基准值中可免费扣减的比例, 随欧盟 ETS 免费配额退坡而逐年下降, 2034 年归零后 CBAM 全额征收。The mark-up applies only to default values (not to actual), rising over time to push firms toward actual data. The CBAM factor sets how much of the benchmark is deductible for free, declining as EU ETS free allocation phases out — reaching 0% in 2034, after which full CBAM applies.

⑬ 使用说明⑬ User Guide

第一步 · 五个下拉定场景Step 1 · Five selectors define the scenario
依次选 年份(定上浮与免费配额系数)、时段(定证书价, 随年份联动)、原产国(按熔炼浇铸地 melt & pour)、HS 编码(先4位大类再8位细码)、数量(报关净重)。生产路线无需操心——默认值口径下由法规按原产国自动锁定。Pick year (sets mark-up & factor), period (certificate price, follows year), origin (melt & pour), HS code (heading then 8-digit), quantity (net mass). The route needs no input — on the default basis it is locked by regulation per origin.
第二步 · 默认值还是实测?Step 2 · Default or actual?
没有经核证的实测数据就用默认值(自动含当年上浮 10/20/30%); 有实测(按 IR 2025/2547 核证)切到实测, 不加上浮, 通常更省钱, 且此时路线解锁、按贵厂实际工艺选。注意: 复杂货物的实测值须已包含适用前体材料的嵌入排放。产业链拆解板块的任一累计卡片可一键填入实测栏做情景测算。Without verified actuals use defaults (year mark-up applied automatically); with verified data (IR 2025/2547) switch to actual — no mark-up, usually cheaper, and the route unlocks to your real process. Note: for complex goods the actual value must already include applicable precursor embedded emissions. Any cumulative card in the value-chain section can be clicked to fill the actual field for scenarios.
第三步 · 读结果Step 3 · Read the results
右侧自动取值面板逐项列出计算用数(默认排放→上浮→基准值(含年份档)→系数→证书价); 结果区给出每吨应清缴、总证书量与欧元成本。数量 ≤50 吨会提示年度豁免核对; 证书价未公布时段按最新已公布价估算并标注。The auto-values panel lists every input figure (default → mark-up → benchmark with year band → factor → price); results show chargeable t, total certificates and euro cost. Qty ≤ 50 t triggers the de-minimis reminder; unpublished periods are estimated at the latest published price, labelled.
进阶 · 高级参数与速查Advanced · parameters & tables
高级参数(默认即官方口径)可调 CSCF、自定义证书价、境外碳价覆盖比例。⑫数据速查可查任意码的两档基准与任意国的默认值; ⑨TRQ 板块看配额与 50% 关税的另一块成本。Advanced parameters (defaults = official basis) expose CSCF, custom certificate price and foreign-carbon-price coverage. Data tables hold both benchmark bands and per-origin defaults; the TRQ section covers the quota/50%-duty side of landed cost.
口径声明Scope note
本工具为估算工具, 数据对齐 IR 2025/2620 与 2025/2621 (经 2026/1740 更正); 正式申报以 CBAM Registry 与届时适用法规为准。首次申报截止 2027-09-30 (覆盖 2026 年)。Estimation tool; data aligned to IR 2025/2620 and 2025/2621 as corrected by 2026/1740. Formal declarations follow the CBAM Registry and applicable law. First declaration due 30 Sep 2027 (for 2026).
